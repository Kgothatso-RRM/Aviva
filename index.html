Established within the Hillcrest Community

Emberton Estate is a charming lifestyle estate within Hillcrest, located just off the N3 with convenient access to schools, shops and daily necessities. This is where you can truly find your home, among a friendly and vibrant community in our established estate.

Emberton offers homes and apartment-style living, for all generations, arranged in village-style blocks, surrounding a large green oval with plenty of facilities for the kids, and a welcoming clubhouse and restaurant, Ray’s Kitchen at the centre.

Feel the country-fresh air and let it invigorate every sense, bringing new possibilities in an established, lifestyle-rich estate that is part of the fabric of Hillcrest’s thriving community.

The Ultimate Hillcrest Scene

Where you live matters, location is everything! Jump into the Hillcrest scene, where the stunning scenery meets your on-the-go lifestyle. Where the neighbourhood buzz meets nature’s tranquillity, and every colour of your life gets its chance to shine. From sweeping hillside views to green-as-can-be landscapes, and from daily essentials to modern luxuries, convenience is always within reach.

Positioned in the heart of a well-established and family-focused node, Emberton is surrounded by some of the area’s most sought-after schools, including Kearsney College, St Mary’s, and the popular Winston Park schooling network, together supporting a thriving community of over 10 000 students nearby.

Beyond education, the lifestyle offering is equally compelling, with weekends spent at Fig Tree Farm, mornings at the Shongweni Farmers Market, and relaxed afternoons on the Winston Park golf course. It’s a location that naturally balances family living, convenience and everyday enjoyment.
